## Title
**Why Did Gold Hit a Record $5,595?**

## Description
Gold shattered its all-time high on January 29, 2026: $5,595/oz. Why the surge? We break it down in 45 seconds.

📊 **Key numbers:**
• +65% in 2025 (best year since 1979)
• 244 tonnes bought by central banks in Q1 2026 alone
• ~800 t/year since 2022, record pace

🎯 **The 3 drivers:**
1. Central banks (China, India, Turkey…) diversifying out of the dollar
2. Weak dollar + start of Fed rate-cut cycle (late 2024)
3. Geopolitical tensions (Middle East, Iran)

➡️ Gold is reclaiming its role as a strategic reserve asset, not just a "safe haven."
